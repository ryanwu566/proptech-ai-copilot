# Customer Interview Pack v1

Use neutral questions and record only consented, necessary information.

## 20-minute script

1. What role do you usually perform in a property transaction?
2. Which steps do you perform before a client meeting?
3. How often do those steps occur in a typical month?
4. Where do rework or missing facts usually appear?
5. Which sources do you need to verify before sharing a result?
6. What would make a preliminary calculation difficult to trust?
7. Which wording or limitation should be clearer?
8. Which current tools do you use for this workflow?
9. Who decides whether a tool can be adopted?
10. What integration or privacy condition would be required?
11. What payment model, if any, would fit your organization?
12. Is there anything you would not want collected in a pilot?

Close by asking whether the participant consents to the structured feedback,
optional contact, and separate anonymous-publication choices. Do not suggest
that the product saves time, improves accuracy, or has been validated.
