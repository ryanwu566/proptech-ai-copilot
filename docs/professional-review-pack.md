# Professional Review Pack v1

Review one recorded product and rule version at a time. Mark each item as
approved for pilot, approved with notes, revision required, out of scope, or
unable to review.

- TaxOracle customer wording, TX001–TX009 trace, source, limitation, and escalation.
- Holding Cost explanation, period, amount, and illustrative boundary.
- Privacy boundary, consent wording, deletion and export behavior.
- Printable report and missing-data language.
- Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean terminology.
- Keyboard, focus, labels, narration, and mobile usability.

Record factual error, legal ambiguity, misleading confidence, missing source,
missing limitation, translation problem, unacceptable professional claim,
reviewed version, scope, required changes, and publication status. A submitted
review is private and is not a public endorsement. Qualification is unverified
until supporting evidence is checked.
