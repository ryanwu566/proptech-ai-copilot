Sources as of 2026-10-10; final review/validation 2026-10-11 (Asia/Taipei); starting SHA `c2ddf1890897f44659b91079a563691fe337295e`. Research only; production **NO-GO** remains unchanged.

The strongest existing value is the combined Taiwan evidence workflow: provenance, explicit unknowns, comparable selection reasons, manual verification/recheck, deterministic comparisons and frozen reports. Protect these semantics. Official provider/runtime acceptance and fleet cost/recovery remain incomplete. [L04](../evidence-verification-checklist-v1.md), [L09](../../frontend_next/lib/workspace/compare-report-model.ts), [L12](../operations/production-guardrails-recovery-v1.md).

The real workflow gaps are integrated viewing records/questions, reusable preferences, explained cross-case trade-offs, multiple commute destinations, unified cash-cost scenarios, grounded Q&A, private documents, multi-device continuity, shared review and monitoring. Generic notes/renovation/manual buyer costs already exist in legacy tools; integrate them instead of claiming they are absent. [L07](../../frontend_next/lib/workspace/finance-model.ts), [L08](../../frontend_next/components/property-case-command-center.tsx).

Shared listing notes/partner review appear in [SRF-02](https://support.redfin.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007862251-Shared-Saved-Searches) and [SHM-01](https://www.homes.com/real-estate-app/); source-linked site Q&A and connected document review appear in [EM-S08](https://land.tech/assess-sites/ai-assistant) and [EM-S05](https://www.orbital.tech/solutions/real-estate-businesses). Adopt the workflow pattern with Taiwan data/permission boundaries. Broad document upload or granular ACL parity was not established in the consumer portals. Beta and discontinued products remain qualified in the registers.

P0: close exact hosted identity/regression, accepted official providers/artifacts, fleet ingress/quota/cost proof, managed recovery/support owners, and current bundle/mobile/print evidence. P1: Decision Room, explicit Preference Profile, deterministic trade-off explanation, bounded multi-destination commute, unified Scenario Simulator and the citation adapter. The [ranked backlog](competitive-gap-backlog-v1.json) records scores, classifications, dependencies, source evidence and acceptance for every proposal.

Three waves, at most two major lanes each: **A** release closure + local Decision Room; **B** staged preference/scenario intelligence + grounded explanation; **C** durable sharing/collaboration + evidence continuity, with documents only after privacy/storage gates. Paid entitlements/support economics remain a separate post-pilot validation decision.

Refuse fake property/safety scores, AI buy/no-buy or invented facts/comps, unauthorized listings/scraping, false valuation confidence or ML-B, generic social/portal expansion, document-based legal clearance and discriminatory neighborhood/school guarantees.
